Line Design Intern, WSP

January to July 2026

I spent six months on WSP’s power line design team, modeling and analyzing transmission lines for utility clients including AEP and National Grid.

  • Modeled transmission structures in PLS-CADD and PLS-POLE, iterating designs to hold up under varying load and weather conditions while balancing material use and performance.
  • Matched conductor tension to LiDAR survey data, attached ADSS fiber, and ran structure, clearance, stringing, and usage reports.
  • Built and reviewed check packages in Bluebeam Revu, Excel, and ProjectWise through a rigorous QA/QC process, following each client’s design standards.
  • Found ways to speed up repetitive work, like using Bluebeam bookmarks and search to pull structure data from 2,900-page calc packages.
